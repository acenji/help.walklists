Canvassing

The door-to-door workflow, screen by screen: find the next door, log what happened, follow up and finish your shift.

The walk list screen

Open a walk list and you see two tabs, List and Map. Below them are your routes (Route 1, Route 2 and so on) and a button to Start shift.

  • Search by name or address.
  • Sort by Route (walking order), Nearest (needs your location) or Score (highest Buyer Score first, where your campaign uses it).
  • In the ranked view, tapping a door jumps to it on the map so you can navigate.

Log a result at a door

  1. Find the door on the list, or tap its pin on the map to open the door sheet.

  2. Tap the result that happened, for example Not home.

  3. A message at the bottom confirms it (“Logged: Not home”) with an Undo button. Keep walking.

The result buttons come from your campaign, so yours may be named differently from the ones in these pictures. If a campaign has none set, the app falls back to Yes, No and Not home. Your manager sets them up in the campaign's Field View.

Change or undo a result

Tapped the wrong button? Tap Undo in the message right away, or tap the correct result: the door changes to it ("Changed to Not home") and the message offers Undo to go back. Undo works for your own results from the last 24 hours. Undoing a door's very first result may not be available yet on every server; the app says so if it is not.

Notes and resident details

Expand the door sheet to Add a note…, call or text the resident, and see details your organization has loaded, such as votes, whether they own the home, registration and precinct, and when the door was last knocked. If your campaign has survey questions, they appear here, and a question marked Answer this first needs an answer before you log the result.

Set a callback

When someone asks you to come back, tap Callback on the door sheet and choose when:

  • In 1 hour
  • This evening (6 PM)
  • Tomorrow morning (9 AM)
  • Tomorrow evening (6 PM)
  • Pick date & time… for any day in the next two weeks, between 8 AM and 8 PM. Times in the past are greyed out.

The phone reminds you at the due time ("Callback due"). Tap the clock button on the map to see Callbacks: overdue ones show how late they are, and each can be changed or cancelled. You can cancel your own callbacks from the last 24 hours (Cancel this callback?, Keep it).

Use the map

The Map tab shows your doors as pins. A white dot means the door is done. At low zoom, doors group into clusters: green means the area is cleared and amber means it is in progress. Several voters can share one door.

  • The round buttons on the right: Center map on my location, the Nearest unvisited door, Callbacks and More map actions.
  • Pin colours: choose Status (the result you logged), Buyer Score or Storm Risk where your campaign has them.
  • Search this route… finds a door by name or address and opens it.

Navigate to the next door

On the door sheet tap Navigate and choose Apple Maps, Google Maps or Waze; the app remembers your choice. For the short walk between doors, tap Walk: a compass arrow points the way ("Hold phone flat • arrow = compass bearing"). Tap Done walking when you arrive.

The Buyer Score

On sales campaigns (Sales, Roofing, Solar, Insurance), each door gets a Buyer Score from 0 to 100 that estimates how likely the household is to buy. Doors are Hot (70 and above), Warm (40 to 69) or Cold. Work the hot doors first to close more in a shift.

  • What it is built from: neighborhood income, home ownership, home value and, for roofing, storm and hail risk. It describes the neighborhood and the home, not the person.
  • Storm risk is historical hail risk for the area: long-term exposure, not a recent storm.
  • A door with no strong signals says so: lower priority, but still worth a knock.

Track your shift

Tap Start shift before you begin. The app counts, on this phone, the results you log until you end the shift. Tap the shift bar to see Shift in progress: doors, conversations, time and doors per hour. Keep going closes the sheet and End shift finishes it.

The shift summary is counted on your phone from what you logged. It is not a server report: a result changed later, or logged on another device, is not reflected.

Partners and the leaderboard

If your manager pairs two canvassers on a street, the map shows how many partners are on the route and which side is yours (Your split: ODD houses or EVEN houses). The door sheet warns you when a partner already knocked a door, so you do not knock it twice. You can hide yourself from partners in Settings. Where your campaign has one, the rank pill (#1 of 3) opens the Leaderboard for the week.

Work offline

You can keep canvassing with no signal. Results, notes, callbacks and photos are saved on the phone and sent when the signal returns. A banner counts what is waiting; tap it to sync. Read more in Mobile App.

Quick tips

Save the map offline before you leave good coverage. Turn on Keep screen on if the phone locks too often. Use Nearest to find the closest unvisited door when you lose your place.
