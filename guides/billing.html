Billing & Plans

How plans are charged, what the trial and the term mean, and how to change or cancel.

Current prices and limits are always shown in the app, on Billing & Usage → Plans. This guide explains how billing works; it does not repeat prices, because they can change.

Where to find billing

Open your account menu and choose Billing & Usage. Only admins and managers can open it; canvassers cannot. It has four tabs:

  • Overview: your plan, status, next billing date, and how much of your plan you have used.
  • Plans: compare plans and start a trial.
  • Payment Methods: Open Billing Portal to update your card and see payment history.
  • Invoices: Invoice History with the date, amount, status and a PDF for each.

Payments are handled by Stripe, by card. WalkLists never stores your card details.

How plans are priced

There are two families. Your campaign vertical decides which one you see.

FamilyForYou payWhat is limited
Sales (field sales, roofing, insurance)Teams of repsPer user, per month (monthly or annual)Number of users, and addresses on some plans
Political (political and canvassing)Campaigns and partiesA flat price per month, with unlimited usersNumber of contacts

Both families start with a free Starter plan ("Free forever", no card, no term). Larger needs, such as 25,000 or more contacts or an Enterprise setup, are quoted: choose Let's talk. The annual option is marked Save 20% and is billed once per year ("Billed annually").

On per-user plans, you choose the number of seats at checkout, up to the plan's user limit, and the Overview shows Seats.

Free trial and contract term

  • 7-day free trial on every paid plan. A card is required up front but not charged for 7 days. Cancel before day 8 and you pay nothing.
  • A banner for managers and admins counts down the trial and says when your card will be charged.
  • Paid plans run on a 12-month term that renews automatically. Choosing monthly or annual only changes how the term is paid. Renewal is at the then-current price, and we email you beforehand.

Read this before choosing monthly. After the trial, a monthly plan is still a 12-month term. Cancelling later stops renewal but does not stop the remaining monthly payments in that term. Annual payments are charged once, up front. Refund terms are in section 6 of our Terms of Service.

What happens at your limits

When you reach a limit the app tells you instead of charging extra. Examples of what you may see:

  • Contacts: "You've hit your plan's lifetime limit of N contacts." The limit is lifetime: contacts you archive or delete still count toward the total.
  • Contact lists: "You've hit your plan's limit of N contact lists."
  • Campaigns: "You've hit your plan's limit of N active campaigns."
  • Team members: "You've hit your plan's limit of N team members."

Each message says how to continue: upgrade, or delete or archive something you no longer need.

Good to know: on a paid plan the usage bar shows a date after "Resets". That date is your billing period. The contact limit itself counts every contact ever added to your organization, and it does not reset. If the bar worries you, contact support.

Start a plan or change it

  1. Open Billing & Usage → Plans.

  2. Choose Monthly or Annual, pick a plan and choose Start Free Trial. You go to a secure Stripe checkout.

  3. Enter your card. After the trial, the plan bills on the schedule you chose.

Changing plans: on Billing & Usage, each paid plan card has a button that opens secure checkout, Open Billing Portal lets you update your card and see payment history, and you can cancel from the same page. How a change in the middle of a term is prorated is handled by Stripe, so contact support before changing a plan mid-term.

Cancel or reactivate

On the Overview choose Cancel Subscription. Pick a reason (required), add feedback if you like, and choose Confirm Cancellation, or Keep Subscription to go back. The dialog shows the exact end date before you confirm.

When you cancelWhat happens
During the trialYou are not charged anything.
Inside your 12-month termYour subscription ends on the date shown and you keep full access until then. If you pay yearly, there is nothing further to pay. If you pay monthly, the remaining monthly payments in the term are still charged.
Subscriptions bought before July 28, 2026They end at the end of the current billing period.

While a cancellation is pending, the Overview shows Access until with the date. To undo it, choose Reactivate Subscription.

If a payment fails

The status badge shows Past Due. Under our Terms we may retry the payment and may suspend access to paid features until the account is brought up to date. Update your card in Payment Methods → Open Billing Portal.

Receipts and invoices

Open Invoices for Invoice History. Each row has a PDF link. A new account shows No invoices yet.
