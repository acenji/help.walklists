Creating Walk Lists

Turn your contacts into routes your team can walk: build the campaign, choose the area, split it, share it.

Before you start

  • Your contacts are imported and in at least one contact list. See CSV Import.
  • Addresses have been placed on the map. Contacts that could not be located, or only with low accuracy, are left out of the walk list. See geocoding quality.
  • You are an admin or manager. Canvassers cannot create campaigns or walk lists.

Create a campaign

Walk lists live inside a campaign. In the left menu choose Campaigns and create one. The wizard has four steps:

  1. Name & Vertical. Give it a Campaign Name and choose a Vertical: Political, Sales, Roofing, Solar or Insurance.

  2. Select Lists. Tick one or more contact lists. Each card shows how many contacts it holds. With no lists yet you see No lists available and a link to import one.

  3. Settings. Default settings are used. You can change them after generation.

  4. Generate. Choose Generate Walk Lists.

Open the Generate page

Open the campaign and choose its Walk Lists tab. It has two sections:

The type is fixed once a draft exists ("Type locked"). To make the other kind, go back to the campaign and start a new one. The campaign's tabs are Settings, Team, Walk Lists, Field View, Results and Activity.

Choose the area

A map preview shows your doors. If the list spans several cities or ZIP codes, All cities and All zips menus let you narrow it. To draw your own area choose Manual lasso (Draw your own), and add more shapes with Draw another lasso. The readout (for example "Lasso · 120 of 480") shows how many doors you captured.

The Generate page does not filter by voter attributes such as party or age. Do that when you build the contact list.

Split the doors into routes

The question is How should the doors be split into routes? Pick a tile; each one shows the number of routes and the smallest to largest route:

  • Neighbourhoods keeps each route inside a neighbourhood, with no road crossings.
  • Balanced sizes gives every route about the same number of doors.
  • Walking time balances routes for tight schedules.
  • Manual lasso lets you draw each route.

Two sliders fine-tune it: Stops per route (5 to 100) and Total routes. They are hidden in Manual lasso mode. If you change an automatic split by hand, a Customised badge appears.

Walking order inside each route

WalkLists orders the doors inside each route automatically. Under Walking Algorithm you can choose:

  • Shortest walking distance
  • Street-by-street (the default)
  • Pick-as-you-go (live; Field Map only)
  • By address (A→Z)
  • Custom order

Under Within each route, Group By (with an optional second level) and Sort Order (Street Number (walking order), Street Name, then Number or Last Name (alphabetical)) set how doors line up.

Odd and even sides of the street

Switch on Split odd/even street numbers to keep the two sides of a street apart. On a PDF it gives separate pages for each side. On a Field Map it pairs two canvassers on opposite sides of each street, and each phone shows which side is theirs.

Fine-tune on the map

After the automatic split you can still change it by hand: select a door and tap another route's marker to move it; Split route; Merge routes; Undo and Redo (⌘Z); drag to reorder doors inside a route; pin a start or end door; or delete a route. Door moves are applied when you click Generate.

PDF options

A PDF has Route, Cover, Layout and Footer settings. Start from Choose a Template or from scratch. You can set a Title, put an HQ number on every page (Campaign Phone, for canvasser safety), choose a cover page (per route, one cover, both or none), add an Assigned to line, and pick a density: Comfortable (about 15 to 20 rows a page), Compact (25 to 30) or Dense (35 to 45). Generation runs Preparing contacts, Clustering routes, Rendering PDF, then Download PDF and Print appear.

Share the Field Map

A Field Map starts as a draft. Choose Generate Field Map to make it live. Then you see Field map live! with the number of routes and stops, and an expiry date.

  • Copy field link gives a link that opens on any phone with no app install and no account.
  • QR code lets people scan it from your screen or a printout.
  • Expires in: 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or Never. The default is 90 days. Once it expires the link shows Share link expired.
  • Rotate link replaces the link. Anyone with the old one needs the new one.

Good to know: the field app also lets a volunteer join with a 6-character code. The web app does not show that code yet, so share the link or QR code. If you need codes for a campaign, contact support. Volunteers can see what a code looks like here: What the code looks like, for volunteers.

Assign people

Open the walk list's Assignees tab (Canvassers on this walklist). You have three ways to add people:

  • Assign existing member: search your organization by name or email. They receive an email and see the walk list in the app.
  • Invite new: enter email, first and last name and choose Send invite. They get an email to set a password and join the walk list.
  • Anonymous share link: copy the link. Anonymous canvassers can knock with it, with no account.

An existing member must already be in your organization, or you are asked to invite them first. Remove someone with the × beside their name. Organization admins can assign anywhere; a campaign manager can assign only on their own campaigns. See Team Management.

Finalize, regenerate, clone and delete

  • Finalize walk list (PDF): locks a ready PDF and counts it under the campaign's walk lists. A finalized list offers Unlock to edit & regenerate and Regenerate with new settings.
  • Each row on the campaign tab has Rename, Clone (the copy is called "Name (copy)"), Delete, Copy share link, Show QR code and Preview as rep.
  • Delete asks first ("Delete this walk list?"). Contact support to recover a deleted walk list within 30 days.
  • Statuses you will see: Draft, Generating, Ready and Locked.

Before you regenerate: check with support before regenerating or deleting a walk list that people are already working, so no logged results are affected.

Good to know

  • Contacts whose address could not be placed, or only placed with low accuracy, are left out of the walk list. Fix them in the contact list and generate again.
  • If some contacts are far from the rest (more than 150 km), they are left out and a banner offers Include them and generate again.
  • If contacts are still being located, generation continues by itself when they finish.
  • A route holds between 5 and 100 stops.
