Account Setup

Sign up, find your settings, invite your team and decide what your canvassers see in the field app.

Create your account

  1. Go to walklists.com/register. The page is titled Create your account.

  2. Enter your Email Address and a Password of at least 8 characters. Your name is optional. Tick the Terms of Service box and choose Create Account. Or choose Continue with Google.

  3. You land on Check your email. We send a verification email and a welcome email. Click the link in the verification email. It works for 3 days, and Send it again sends a new one.

Addresses on throw-away email services, or on domains that cannot receive mail, are not accepted. If you sign up from a shared office network and see a "too many attempts" message, wait a few minutes and try again.

If you chose a paid plan on the pricing page first, signup takes you to a card page for a 7-day trial before email verification. See Billing & Plans.

Your first login

After you verify your email, you arrive on your dashboard. With no campaigns yet, a Get started in 4 steps card shows the path: Import Contacts, Create a Contact List, Launch a Campaign and Generate Walk Lists. Once you have a campaign, the card is replaced by a snapshot of your team's activity. Follow it in the Quick Start Guide.

Your organization

WalkLists creates your organization for you when you sign up, and you are its Owner. The Owner cannot be removed. The organization is named after you by default, for example "Ana Rivera's Organization".

To rename your organization, open Account → Company in the left menu. The page has Company Name, Company Address, Time zone, Contact Email, Country and your company logo. Change what you need and choose Save.

Where your settings live

Open the Account group in the left menu. What you see depends on your role. The pages you will use most:

  • Company: organization name, address, time zone, contact email, country and logo (see above).
  • My Profile: first name, last name, email and photo, and Change Password (current password, new password, confirm). Deleting your account starts here too, under Danger Zone.
  • Staff: invite and manage people. See Team Management.
  • Billing & Usage: your plan, usage and invoices. See Billing & Plans.
  • Providers, PDF & Print, API keys, Voter data terms and Webhooks: connections and advanced options for managers and admins.

The moon icon in the top bar switches between light and dark mode.

Choose a vertical for each campaign

The vertical is set per campaign, in Campaign Settings → General → Vertical: Political, Sales, Roofing, Solar or Insurance. It changes how doors are prioritised and which tools the canvasser sees:

VerticalDoors are prioritised byIn the field app
PoliticalHigh-propensity voters firstList, map, results, callbacks
Sales, Insurance, SolarHighest lead score firstPlus deals, appointments, Buyer Score
RoofingHighest damage score firstPlus roof measurement, deals, appointments, Buyer Score

The follow-up rule in Targeting offers Retry not-home after 2 days, Schedule callback, Schedule estimate visit, No automatic follow-up and Custom rules.

Decide what canvassers see: Field View

Open the campaign and choose the Field View tab. The editor is called Field view configuration, and a Live preview shows what a rep sees in the field. Changes save automatically when you leave a field.

  • Survey questions: up to five per campaign. Types: Yes / No, Choose one, 1-5 rating and Short text.
  • Primary status buttons: the result buttons on the door sheet. The first four slots are universal and locked. Slots five and six can be swapped for the buttons that suit your vertical.
  • Custom marker indicators: a dot, ring or icon on the map pin based on a column in your contacts.

The field app reads the button names and questions from here, so a rename on the web appears on every canvasser's phone. A walk list can also carry its own Field View override.

Not available yet: the call-script selector in Campaign Settings is switched off for now ("No script selected"). Do not plan on scripts for the next canvass.

Roles

There are three kinds of people: admins, managers and canvassers. The Owner is an admin who also owns the organization. Canvassers cannot open billing, contacts, lists, campaigns or integrations. Full details and how to invite each kind are in Team Management.

Sign-in options

  • Email and password, with Forgot Your Password? on the login page.
  • Google, on the web and in the field app.
  • Apple, in the field app on iPhone.
  • Single sign-on (SAML) for organizations that use Okta, Azure AD or OneLogin. See SSO Setup. Our team sets up the connection for you; contact support to start.

Reset your password

  1. On the login page choose Forgot Your Password?.

  2. On Reset your password, enter your email address and choose Send Reset Link.

  3. Open the email and click the link within 3 days. If you are signed in and just want a new password, use Account → My Profile → Change Password.

Delete your account

You can ask for your account and data to be deleted without signing in, at walklists.com/delete-account. The field app links to the same page from Settings.

  1. Choose Request account deletion and complete the check.

  2. We email you a confirmation link. Nothing happens until you click it.

  3. After you confirm, deletion is scheduled for 30 days later, and you can cancel it during that time.

If you own an organization that has other members, transfer ownership or remove the members first. If you need written confirmation that your data has been deleted, contact support.
