Deals, Appointments & Photos

For sales, roofing, solar and insurance campaigns: turn a conversation at the door into a quote, a signature or an appointment.

Who sees this? These tools appear on campaigns that have a deal setup (the sales verticals). On a political campaign the door sheet has result buttons only. The names you see, such as the deal button, come from your campaign, so yours may differ from the pictures.

Open the deal form

Open a door's sheet and pull it up. Two big buttons sit under the photos: the deal button (in these pictures Roof inspection & quote) and Schedule appointment.

Capture a deal

The form is a stepper. A roofing campaign has five steps; yours may have fewer, depending on its setup. Back and Next move between steps, and you can Cancel at any time.

  1. Lead. First name, last name, email and phone.

  2. Inspection. Measure the roof, choose the pitch and complexity, note any visible storm damage, add inspection notes and photos.

  3. Insurance. The insurance carrier and whether a claim was filed.

  4. Quote. Scope (for example tear-off, re-roof, repairs), materials, tier, financing and the total.

  5. Signature. The customer signs with a finger on the phone. Type the signer's full name, hand the phone over, then tap Submit signature.

Measure a roof

  1. On the Inspection step tap Tap to measure roof. The app uses your location to find the building and pulls the roof footprint and a satellite photo.

  2. Choose the roof pitch (Flat, 4/12 up to 12/12).

  3. Choose the roof complexity (waste): Simple +10% or Complex +15%.

  4. The app shows the estimate in roofing squares and fills it into the quote. The line under it shows the maths: footprint × pitch multiplier × waste.

It is an estimate. The footprint is the flat outline of the building. Pitch and waste turn it into the material you would really need. If location is off or the lookup fails, the app asks you to enter the size by hand.

Solar estimate

On solar campaigns the inspection step also offers a Solar estimate: choose which way the panels face, how much shade there is and the customer's monthly electric bill. The app returns the number of panels, the share of the bill offset, yearly savings and an estimated 25-year saving, with monthly production. If panels are already on the roof, it asks you to confirm before estimating.

Book an appointment

  1. On the door sheet tap Schedule appointment.

  2. Choose the Type (your campaign defines the list; for example Inspection, Closer visit, Install or Follow-up), a Day and a Time. Times in the past are greyed out.

  3. Check the customer's name, add an email if you want a confirmation sent, and add a phone number and notes for the team.

  4. Tap Book appointment.

If you entered an email, the customer gets a confirmation. With no email, the screen says so ("not confirmed by email"). With no signal the appointment is saved on the phone and booked when you are back online.

Photos

Use Take to open the camera or Add to choose from your library. The first time, your phone asks for permission. Photos you take without a signal wait on the phone ("1 photo saved on this phone, waiting to send") and upload later. If the phone's photo storage for the app is full, connect to send the waiting photos and take the picture again.

After the signature

When the agreement is signed the app shows Agreement signed, generates the contract and emails it to the customer if you entered an email. You can also text the customer a link to it. If you have no signal when you finish, the deal stays on the phone and sends itself later, but the signature itself needs a signal: try signing again once you have one.
