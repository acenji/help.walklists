Quick Start Guide

Go from zero to canvassing in under 10 minutes.

1

Create your account

Go to walklists.com/register and sign up with your email or Google account. Verify your email to unlock all features.

2

Upload your contact list

Click New Walk List and upload a CSV file with your contacts. At minimum, include columns for name and address. WalkLists will automatically geocode the addresses and plot them on the map.

3

Review the map

Once geocoding completes, your contacts appear as pins on an interactive map. You can filter, sort, and group contacts by any column in your data.

4

Assign canvassers

Invite team members by email. Assign them territories or specific contact lists. They'll receive an invite to download the mobile app.

5

Start canvassing

Canvassers open the mobile app, see their assigned contacts on the map with optimized walking routes, and log interactions at each door.
