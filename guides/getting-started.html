Quick Start Guide

Go from signup to your first door. Plan about ten minutes for the setup and a few more for geocoding.

WalkLists works in two places. You prepare the work on the web app (contacts, campaigns, walk lists). Your canvassers do the work on the field app on their phones. This guide follows the first walk list from end to end.

Set up in six steps

  1. Create your account.

    Go to walklists.com/register. Enter your email and a password (at least 8 characters), accept the Terms of Service and choose Create Account, or choose Continue with Google.

    If you signed up with email, open the message we send and click the verification link. The app stays closed until you do. The link works for 3 days, and Send it again sends a new one. More about accounts and settings.

  2. Import your contacts.

    On your dashboard, the Get started in 4 steps card starts with Import Contacts: upload a CSV or add contacts by hand. WalkLists checks each address and places it on the map. CSV import guide.

  3. Create a contact list.

    Lists group your contacts. A campaign builds its walk lists from the lists you pick, so decide which doors belong together.

  4. Launch a campaign.

    Open Campaigns and create one. The wizard has four steps: Name & Vertical, Select Lists, Settings and Generate. The vertical (Political, Sales, Roofing, Solar or Insurance) decides which tools your canvassers see. Details.

  5. Generate your walk lists.

    In the campaign, open the Walk Lists tab and generate a Field Map (the digital version your team opens on a phone) or a PDF to print. Choose the area, split the doors into routes and generate. Creating walk lists.

  6. Get your team canvassing.

    Share the field link or QR code, or assign people to the walk list. Canvassers can open the link in a phone browser with nothing to install, or use the field app. Field app guide.

Start small. Import a short list of 50 to 100 doors first and walk it yourself. You will see exactly what your canvassers see before you send the whole team out.

What your canvassers will do

On the phone, a canvasser joins the walk list, works through the doors on the list or the map, taps a result at each door, and sets callbacks for people who asked to be contacted again. Everything is saved on the phone first, so a weak signal never loses a result. The Canvassing guide walks through each screen.
