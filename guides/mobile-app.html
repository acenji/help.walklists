Mobile App

Everything you need before your first door: signing in, joining a walk list, your language and your settings.

The WalkLists field app is where canvassers do the work. It runs on iPhone and Android, shows your doors on a list and a map, and keeps every result safe on the phone until there is signal to send it.

No app yet? Your manager can also share a field link. Opening it in a phone browser needs no install and no account.

Ways to sign in

The first screen offers four choices.

  • Join with code: for volunteers and canvassers who were given a 6-character code. No account needed. See the next section.
  • Continue with Apple (iPhone only) and Continue with Google: sign in with an account you already have.
  • Sign in with email: use your WalkLists email and password. Forgot your password? sends a reset link. If your organization uses single sign-on, the app tells you to use Apple or Google instead.

Join with a code

  1. On the sign-in screen, tap Join with code.

  2. Type the 6-character code your organizer gave you. Codes use letters and numbers, never the letters I, L, O or U, so a 0 is always a zero. Lowercase is fine, and you can paste the whole code into the first box.

  3. Tap Join walklist. You go straight to that one walk list.

Your walk lists

If you signed in with an account, My Walklists lists every walk list assigned to you, with its doors and routes. Tap Open to start. A walk list that is still being built says Preparing…. With nothing assigned you see No walklists assigned yet: your manager assigns you turf, and it shows up here. Pull down to refresh.

Pick your language

The app speaks 20 languages, and each person chooses their own. Two people on the same campaign can use different languages.

  1. Tap the globe button at the top of almost any screen (it shows your language code, for example EN). You can do this before you sign in, too.

  2. Pick your language. The change is immediate, and the app remembers it on this phone.

You can also change it later in Settings → Language. Arabic reads right to left, and the app restarts once when you switch to or from it. Names that come from your campaign, such as the result buttons, appear as your organization wrote them.

Settings

Open Settings from the footer of the walk list (tap your name at the bottom).

  • Appearance: System, Light or Dark.
  • Language: see above.
  • Show walking partners on my map: turn it off to hide yourself from your partners too. They still see the doors you have done, and your organization still sees your activity.
  • Save mobile data: on mobile data only, the app uses smaller photos, updates partners more slowly and warns you before large map downloads.
  • Keep screen on: stops the phone from locking while you canvass. It uses more battery.
  • Help & support, Privacy, Terms, Sign out and Delete account.

Messages

Team chat is available when you sign in with an account. From My Walklists open Messages, start one with New, search for a teammate, and send text or photos. You see when a message was sent and seen, and when someone is typing. Messages are not available to volunteers who joined with a code.

Working without a signal

Results, notes, photos, deals and appointments you save are kept on the phone first and sent when you have signal. You do not need to do anything.

  • A banner shows how many items are waiting. Tap it to sync.
  • If the server refuses an item, the banner says so, so nothing disappears silently.
  • To keep a map for offline use, open the map menu and tap Save offline. On mobile data the app asks first, because a big map download can use a lot of data.
  • Signing an agreement needs a signal. The deal is kept on the phone, and you try signing again when the signal is back.

Get help

In Settings → Help & support you can open the Help Center, Email support (with diagnostics) or Share diagnostics. The sheet shows exactly what would be sent. It contains no password, sign-in token, email address or door data.

Troubleshooting

My doors do not appear

Pull down to refresh. If the walk list says Preparing…, it is still being built. If nothing is assigned, ask your manager.

The map says it needs my location

The map and the nearest-door sort need location. Allow it for WalkLists in your phone's settings. Without it you can still work from the list.

I switched language and the layout flipped

Arabic reads right to left. Switching to or from Arabic restarts the app once to change the layout direction.

I want to start over

Use Sign out in Settings to sign out of this phone, then sign in or join again.
